Bedfords Home For World Class Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Jiu-Jitsu is more than a sport. It's a way to build confidence, discipline, fitness, and friendships.

At Bernardo Faria Academy in Bedford, MA, we offer world-class Jiu-Jitsu instruction for kids, teens, and adults in a safe, welcoming, and family-friendly environment. Our programs are designed to help students build confidence, improve fitness, learn self-defense, and develop their Jiu-Jitsu skills.

Whether you're stepping on the mats for the very first time or you're an experienced practitioner looking to improve your game, our instructors and community are here to help you learn, grow, and enjoy every step of your Jiu-Jitsu journey.
